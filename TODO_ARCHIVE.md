# todo_md — archived tasks (DONE and CANCELED)

Active operational tasks live in `TODO.md`; deferred tasks live in `TODO_PLAN.md`.

update a task's status badge or move a completed task from `TODO.md` to `TODO_ARCHIVE.md`.
